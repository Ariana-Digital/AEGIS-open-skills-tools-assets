# AI Has a Supply Chain: episode and download guide

Updated 1 October 2026. The series now closes with two episodes after Episode 6. The original Episodes 7–12 have been consolidated into Episodes 7 and 8.

Download names are stable. Version 1.0.0 packages retain their original episode labels, including labels inside the tools. Choose by topic using this guide.

| Revised series | Topic and use | Existing download |
|---|---|---|
| Episode 6 · Control | Review rights, evidence and operating duties | [Control Review](https://github.com/Ariana-Digital/AEGIS-open-skills-tools-assets/raw/refs/heads/main/downloads/episode-06-control-review-v1.0.0.zip) |
| Episode 7 · Before You Buy More Compute | Compare supplied candidate results against workload requirements | [Placement Test](https://github.com/Ariana-Digital/AEGIS-open-skills-tools-assets/raw/refs/heads/main/downloads/episode-07-placement-test-v1.0.0.zip) |
| Episode 7 · Before You Buy More Compute | Review the dependencies behind a conditional service date | [Delivery Gates](https://github.com/Ariana-Digital/AEGIS-open-skills-tools-assets/raw/refs/heads/main/downloads/episode-08-delivery-gates-v1.0.0.zip), originally labeled Episode 8 |
| Episode 8 · Who Owns AI After Approval? | Review change authority, evidence retrieval and conditional decisions | No new browser workflow released |

Placement Test and Delivery Gates are separate tools. Carry the same workload identifier into both and attach their exported briefs to the same review. Cross-tool import is not supported. The tools organize supplied evidence; they do not benchmark models, diagnose infrastructure or approve production.

## Original material

Original 7 (placement), 8 (delivery) and 9 (throughput) now inform the revised Episode 7. Original 10 (operations), 11 (evidence) and 12 (board decision) inform the revised Episode 8. Selected visual downloads for Episodes 1–6 are unchanged.
