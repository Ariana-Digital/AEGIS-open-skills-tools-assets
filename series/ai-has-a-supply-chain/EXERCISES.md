# Five small reviews

Use one real business service and redacted evidence references. These exercises organize a discussion; they do not grant approval. Blank means unknown. Assign an owner and a next action to each unresolved item.

## Episode 1: trace one dependency

Choose a business outcome. Trace it through workflow, model, identity, data, hosting, compute, network, power and operations. For each dependency, record the accountable party and what happens if it becomes unavailable. Finish with one evidence request for the least understood dependency.

Copy this record: `Service / dependency / owner / evidence reference / failure consequence / recovery or exit / next action / due date`.

## Episode 2: qualify a capacity claim

Quote one capacity claim exactly and record its source date. Identify what it establishes: an announcement, commercial backing, permissions, power, constructed capacity or accepted access for your workload. These states can advance in parallel. Obtain a customer-specific commitment for service, region, quantity, timing, acceptance and fallback before treating it as usable capacity.

Copy this record: `Exact claim / source and date / actor / evidence state / service and region / quantity and unit / customer commitment / open gate / remedy / owner / recheck date`.

## Episode 3: price accepted work

Pick one period and one accepted business unit. Reconcile provider charges, commitments, platform costs, engineering, review, operations and allocated shared cost. Divide the reconciled cost by accepted volume from the same scope and period. Retain rejected work and retries in cost. If accepted volume is zero or unknown, report that; do not divide by raw calls as a substitute.

Copy this record: `Workload / period / accepted-unit definition / cost lines and sources / allocation rule / accepted volume and evidence / cost per accepted unit / benefit evidence / owner`.

The included healthcare image uses invented $12,000 total cost and 7,500 accepted items: $1.60 per item. These are not observed customer results or a cost benchmark.

## Episode 4: reconcile a financing claim

Select one headline and identify its legal entity, instrument, asset, date and status. Separate a target from a commitment, and a commitment from deployed cash. Check overlap before adding two figures. Read the contracts to identify who bears lower utilization, delivery delay and lower residual value.

Copy this record: `Claim / source and date / entity / instrument / asset / gross and net cash / status / overlap / guarantees / downside holder / missing evidence / owner`.

This is an evidence exercise, not an investment recommendation.

## Episode 5: run a 20-minute continuity tabletop

Use a fictional quality-vision service at a US plant. The provider endpoint is unavailable and goods await release. Before starting, name who may hold, stop or authorize a validated manual process. Nobody should operate real machinery or change production during this exercise.

| Exercise minute | Inject | Ask the participants |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Model endpoint unavailable | What business decision stops? What is the safe state? |
| 3 | Both regional deployments share identity | Does the alternate route still work? What evidence shows it? |
| 7 | Manual capacity is half the planned amount | When does the queue exceed safe operating capacity? |
| 11 | Review decisions lag the image record | Which goods have complete evidence and release authority? |
| 15 | The recovery route uses a different model version | Who accepts changed behavior before release resumes? |
| 20 | Debrief | Which assumption failed, who owns the repair and when will it be retested? |

The 45-minute recovery-time and 8-minute recovery-point figures in the visuals are illustrative targets, not standards or recommendations. Derive real targets from the actual business impact and tested recovery capability.

Copy this record: `Dependency / observed failure / approved degraded mode / trained capacity / evidence state / authority / recovery test / next action / owner / due date`.

### Episode 5 carousel text companion

1. A fictional plant's model endpoint fails while 412 units await release. Choose continue, manual inspection or hold, then establish whether that choice is authorized and can run.
2. Two regions can share identity, routing, policy, data and model-access dependencies. Test the whole recovery path.
3. Image capture, scoring, review, product release and shipment form the business service. An available endpoint alone does not restore it.
4. A manual route needs trained staff and current instructions. A hold needs space and authority. Stale AI output is not a generic fallback.
5. Recovery-time and recovery-point targets must reflect people, evidence and physical capacity as well as technology. The depicted targets are invented teaching inputs.
6. Run the timed exercise above and capture the response to each inject.
7. Choose one dependency to test, one approved degraded mode and a named approval path.

The separate plant illustration labels a physical **Gate 412**. It is a different visual convention from the carousel's **412 units**. Do not treat the two numbers as one event record.
